# DemonX Forge 0.14.0

Number entry on the shape, picking overlapping shapes, a right-click menu and a shortcuts list.

- **Size boxes sit on the shape:** while you draw, the width and height of a rectangle (or the diameter of a circle, or the length and angle of a line) are boxes on the shape itself. Type a number to replace it, Backspace to clear it, Tab to go to the next box, Enter to place. The shape on the screen follows what you type.
- **Overlapping shapes:** a rectangle and a circle that cross are now three separate shapes (the overlap, the rest of the rectangle, the rest of the circle). Move the mouse over them and each lights up; click one, press E, and only that piece is extruded.
- **Right-click menu:** in the view it offers what you can do with what is selected (extrude a picked shape, press pull or drill a picked face, the sketch tools while sketching), plus Undo, Redo and the shortcuts.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** the `?` button in the header (or the `?` key) lists every key and mouse action.

- **The DemonX look:** the header now carries the DX devil-horn mark and the DemonX Forge wordmark, and the browser tab has the DX icon.

**Not there yet:** boxes for arcs and slots, a radial marking menu like Fusion's, your own shortcut keys.
**Tested:** unit tests (88) and nineteen real-browser checks against the real engine on Linux, including typing and clearing in the boxes, and extruding the overlap of a rectangle and a circle. **Not tested:** the Windows zip on a Windows PC.

Download `DemonX-Forge-0.14.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract it to `C:\`, run `Start DemonX Forge.bat`.

# DemonX Forge 0.13.1

Drawing the way Fusion does, from the first test of 0.13.0.

- **Sizes while you draw:** a rectangle shows its width and height, a circle its diameter, a line its length, on the shape and in boxes under the view. No need to click into a box first.
- **Tab between the boxes:** Tab goes into the first box and on to the next (Width, Height; Length, Angle). Type a value to fix it, leave the other to follow the mouse, press Enter to place the shape.
- **Draw by dragging:** press at one corner, drag, release at the other. Clicking twice still works.
- **Pick a shape, then E:** after Finish Sketch, click the shape you want in the view and press E (or Revolve, Sweep, ...). The shapes of sketches that have not been extruded yet are shaded so you can see what to click.

**Not there yet:** the size boxes sit under the view, not on the shape; boxes for arcs and slots; Shift-click to pick several shapes in the model view.
**Tested:** unit tests (84) and nineteen real-browser checks against the real engine on Linux, including a mouse drag and Tab between the boxes. **Not tested:** the Windows zip on a Windows PC.

Download `DemonX-Forge-0.13.1-windows-x64.zip`, extract it to `C:\`, run `Start DemonX Forge.bat`.

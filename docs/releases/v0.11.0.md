# DemonX Forge 0.11.0

Move / Copy, Split Body, a real Hole dialog (and editing holes), Thread and Rib.

- **Hole (H)** is now a dialog with a live preview: click a face, check the position (it is filled in from your click and you can type exact coordinates), set the diameter and depth (empty goes right through), and choose simple, counterbore or countersink.
- **Edit a hole:** double-click it in the timeline. The same dialog opens with its numbers; change them and the hole follows.
- **Move / Copy:** move a body by X, Y, Z and/or turn it about a world axis; Copy keeps the original.
- **Split Body:** cut a body in two along an X, Y or Z plane at a position you type.
- **Thread:** click the round wall of a hole or boss, choose a size (empty picks the one that fits). A cosmetic thread marks the hole in the timeline; tick Modeled to cut the real thread. A size that does not fit is refused with the reason.
- **Rib:** grow a wall from a line in a sketch: thickness, depth and flip.
- The ribbon now scrolls sideways when the window is too narrow for every button.

**Not there yet:** editing the other new tools from the timeline, hole positions by dragging, tapped holes from a table, STEP import, a section DXF.
**Tested:** unit tests (81) and fourteen real-browser checks against the real engine on Linux. **Not tested:** the Windows zip on a Windows PC.

Download `DemonX-Forge-0.11.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract it to `C:\`, run `Start DemonX Forge.bat`.

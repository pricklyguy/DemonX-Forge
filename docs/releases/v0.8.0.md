# DemonX Forge 0.8.0

Shell and Revolve.

- **Shell** (SOLID tab, MODIFY): click the face or faces to leave open, type a wall thickness. The part is hollowed inward and shows the result while you type. A wall that cannot fit is refused with the engine's reason and the part is left alone.
- **Revolve** (SOLID tab, CREATE): finish a sketch, press Revolve, pick the profiles, choose the axis (the sketch's X or Y axis, a line of the sketch, or a world axis) and an angle up to 360 degrees. Operation (new body, join, cut, intersect) as for Extrude. Live preview, OK / Cancel, one undo step.
- Fixed: a late preview after OK could leave two undo steps for one edit.

**Not there yet:** editing a revolve, shell or press pull from the timeline, dragging for press/pull, mirror, patterns, sweep, loft, DXF of a face, STEP import.
**Tested:** unit tests (73) and nine real-browser checks against the real engine on Linux. **Not tested:** the Windows zip on a Windows PC.

Download `DemonX-Forge-0.8.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract it to `C:\`, run `Start DemonX Forge.bat`.

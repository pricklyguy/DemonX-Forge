# DemonX Forge 0.18.0

Mesh to Solid, more robust.

- **Leaning walls.** A slab whose walls all lean the same way (a drafted block, a chamfer along an edge, a tapered boss or hole) is rebuilt as an extrude with a taper.
- **A second direction.** What does not fit the main direction (a countersink, a hole through the base of an L-shaped bracket, a boss on the side) is found as a group of faces of its own and rebuilt as a cut or a join along the direction that suits it, with a taper if it is a cone. The converter tries each of X, Y and Z as the main direction and uses the one that needs the fewest extra features.
- **Still checked:** the rebuilt volume must match the mesh's to within 1%, otherwise everything is undone and the next way is tried, then the engine's prism converter. One Ctrl+Z takes it all back.
- **The demo bracket now converts** (an L-shaped section with holes through its base). It was the example that was refused in 0.16 and 0.17.

**Still refused (with the reason, nothing changed):** sculpted shapes, spheres, walls that lean unevenly (a fillet along an edge), a part chamfered in two directions at once, and anything the engine cannot extrude with a taper (a tapered profile where an arc meets a line at a corner).

**Tested:** the real engine for a drafted block, a plate with a countersunk hole, an L-shaped bracket with holes through its base, a bracket with a boss and a hole through its wall, and a drafted boss; each compared by volume with the part it was exported from; the browser check `e2e/meshimport.mjs` (the demo bracket rebuilt, undo, a double chamfer refused). **Not tested:** meshes from other programs, meshes of hundreds of thousands of triangles (the code avoids slow loops but its speed is not measured), Windows.

# DemonX Forge 0.26.0

Three fixes from testing 0.25.0.

- **Fix: "this face can't keep an attachment" when sketching on a face.** The engine refuses to attach a sketch to a face whose history it cannot trace (a part that has been edited a lot). Forge now falls back to an ordinary sketch on the same flat plane. The sketch opens and works as normal; the only difference is that it will not follow that face if you later change the model underneath it.
- **Faces light up when you choose a sketch plane.** Create Sketch used to show only the three origin planes. The flat face under the pointer now lights up too, and a click sketches on it (it was already possible, but there was no sign of it).
- **Reach another face's height.** In Extrude, click a flat face and the Distance becomes the distance that reaches it, so the new shape ends up level with that face. In Press Pull (and Offset Face), Alt + click the face to reach; a plain click still changes which face is being moved. The face has to be parallel to the one being moved. The distance can still be dragged or typed afterwards.

**Tested:** real engine and real browser (`e2e/attach.mjs`: the engine's refusal is simulated and the sketch still opens on the right plane; `e2e/snap.mjs`: a face lights up, Extrude and Press Pull reach a face 5 mm higher). **Not tested:** the real engine refusal on your part (I could not make the engine give it on a simple model, so it is simulated), faces that are not parallel to the one being moved, Windows.

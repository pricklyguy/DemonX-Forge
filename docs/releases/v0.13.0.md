# DemonX Forge 0.13.0

Scale, Coil, Pipe, Emboss, and dragging for Press Pull and Extrude.

- **Drag the arrow:** in Press Pull and Extrude an arrow appears in the view. Drag its tip to set the distance (0.5 mm steps, hold Shift for 0.1); the box and the preview follow, and typing a number moves the arrow. Drag past the face to push in (Press Pull) or reverse (Extrude).
- **Scale** (MODIFY): a factor (or different factors for X, Y and Z), about the middle of the body or the origin.
- **Coil** (CREATE): wire diameter, round or square section, and exactly two of turns, height and pitch; axis and start point. No body is needed.
- **Pipe** (CREATE): a round pipe along a path sketch, solid or hollow.
- **Emboss** (CREATE): raise a sketch profile off the face it was drawn on, or cut it in (Deboss).

**Not there yet:** dragging for fillet radius and hole position, editing mirror / patterns / combine / move from the timeline, text emboss, FreeCAD and Rhino import.
**Tested:** unit tests (84) and eighteen real-browser checks against the real engine on Linux, including mouse drags of the arrow. **Not tested:** the Windows zip on a Windows PC, a touch screen.

Download `DemonX-Forge-0.13.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract it to `C:\`, run `Start DemonX Forge.bat`.

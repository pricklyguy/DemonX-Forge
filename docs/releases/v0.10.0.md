# DemonX Forge 0.10.0

Offset Face, Draft, Combine, and DXF of a face.

- **Offset Face** (MODIFY): click one or more flat faces and type a distance; positive moves them out, negative moves them in.
- **Draft** (MODIFY): click the wall faces, choose the neutral plane (the level that stays put: bottom or top of the body, or an origin plane) and an angle. A 5 degree draft on a 10 mm wall moves its far edge by 0.87 mm.
- **Combine** (MODIFY): choose the target body, click the tool bodies, then Join, Cut or Intersect, with an option to keep the tool bodies.
- **DXF of a face:** click a flat face, then File > Export > Selected face. You get the face's outline and holes as a DXF in millimetres, ready for DemonX Design (it opened there as the outline and the hole, with no warnings). Nothing is added to your design by doing this.
- Each tool has a live preview, OK / Cancel (Enter / Esc) and one undo step.

**Not there yet:** a section DXF (a cut through a body), pocket and hole depths in the DXF, picking the draft's neutral plane from a face, editing these from the timeline, STEP import.
**Tested:** unit tests (79) and thirteen real-browser checks against the real engine on Linux; the DXF was also read by DemonX Design's own importer. **Not tested:** the Windows zip on a Windows PC.

Download `DemonX-Forge-0.10.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract it to `C:\`, run `Start DemonX Forge.bat`.

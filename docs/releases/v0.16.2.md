# DemonX Forge 0.16.2

The DemonX palette.

- New colours for dark and light: slate base, the logo's teal as the accent, the logo's copper for warnings, a bluer information colour, and a separate colour for the 3D view's background. The values are the ones in the palette file the owner supplied; the greys between them (second panel colour, lines, muted text) are worked out from it.
- The warm browns and greens of the old palette are gone from the page, the ribbon, the browser tree and the 3D view.

**Tested:** looked at in a real browser in both themes (the bracket, the ribbon, the browser tree, the timeline). **Not tested:** contrast checks beyond looking, Windows.

If you like it, the same variables (`--canvas`, `--bg`, `--panel`, `--panel2`, `--line`, `--text`, `--muted`, `--accent`, `--accent-text`, `--amber`, `--cyan`, `--danger`, `--ok`, at the top of `web/src/styles.css`) can be copied to DemonX CNC and Design.

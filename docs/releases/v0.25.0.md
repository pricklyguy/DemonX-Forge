# DemonX Forge 0.25.0

Two fixes from testing 0.24.0.

- **Fix: a strip sketched along the edge of a face could not be extruded.** A sketch made on a face already shows the face's outline. A rectangle drawn along one of its edges has lines lying on top of that outline, and the shapes were not worked out correctly there, so the strip could not be clicked and Extrude said "Select a profile". Lines on top of each other are now split where the other one starts and ends, and the strip is selected and extruded like any other shape.
- **Fix: orbiting stopped at the top (and bottom) view.** Shift + middle-button drag now turns the view all the way round, past the top and bottom, so you can look at the edge between the top and the back. The view cube follows. Home and the cube faces still reset the view upright.

**Tested:** real engine and real browser (`e2e/strip.mjs`: a 40 x 30 block, a 40 x 4 strip along the back edge of its top face, the two shapes, clicking the strip, extruding it 5 mm; orbit past the top view with Shift + middle drag), a unit test for the overlapping lines (`regions.test.ts`), and the other e2e checks that touch the viewport and sketching. **Not tested:** Windows, a real mouse, and the arrow buttons around Fusion's view cube (not built; say if you want them).
